Risk factors: dose reduction

Suggested reductions follow the multifactorial approach of Rosenberg et al. (2004). Ticking a factor fills the middle of its range; every value is editable. The highest ticked reduction applies to all maxima — factors are not added together. Starting values are approximate: confirm them against the paper and local guidance.

Adults 18 and over only. Every maximum is a ceiling, not a target. Verify each calculation independently before giving anything.

Doses given

Toxicity is treated as additive: each dose counts as a fraction of its own drug's maximum, and the fractions are summed. Topical airway lidocaine is counted against 9 mg/kg lean body weight. Summing assumes everything was given at once, so it never underestimates. Infusions (bupivacaine, levobupivacaine, ropivacaine) are tracked separately against 24-hour maximums. Maximum = min(mg/kg × dosing weight, ceiling) × (1 − reduction).

Sequence (DAS 2020 ATI)

  • Sedation: optional and minimal; the patient must stay cooperative.
  • Topicalisation: lidocaine, tested before instrumentation; stay under 9 mg/kg LBW.
  • Oxygenation: high-flow nasal oxygen throughout.
  • Performance: limit attempts to 3, plus 1 by a more experienced operator.
  • Consider an antisialagogue such as glycopyrronium before topicalisation.

Immediate management

  1. Stop injecting. Call for help. Get the lipid emulsion.
  2. Airway, 100% oxygen, consider mild hyperventilation (raising pH may help); IV access.
  3. Seizures: benzodiazepine first; small doses of propofol or thiopental only if haemodynamically stable.
  4. Cardiac arrest: CPR and ALS. Arrhythmias can be very refractory; continue CPR for a prolonged period (may exceed 1 hour). Consider cardiopulmonary bypass/ECMO.
  5. ASRA: reduce adrenaline to ≤1 mcg/kg boluses; avoid vasopressin, calcium-channel blockers, beta-blockers and further local anaesthetic.
  6. Avoid lidocaine as an antiarrhythmic. Do not use propofol as a lipid substitute.
  7. Report the case and arrange follow-up (e.g. pancreatitis screen after lipid).
